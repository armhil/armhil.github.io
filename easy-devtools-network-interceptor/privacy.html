Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026. This policy covers version 2.0 of Easy DevTools – Network Interceptor, developed by Arman Hilmioglu.

Data processed on your device

The extension reads HTTP/HTTPS request URLs and tab identifiers to show recent requests and apply your URL redirect and blocking rules. URLs may contain personal or sensitive information. Recent request records are kept in Chrome session storage, limited to 50 entries, cleared when you change the active tab, and removed when the browser session ends.

Your interception rules, replacement URLs, master switch setting, and dismissed notices are stored locally in the browser profile. The popup also uses local storage as a local cache. No browser sync service is used by the extension.

No developer collection or analytics

The extension does not send request logs, browsing activity, or rules to the developer. It includes no analytics, advertising, remote code, or developer-operated collection service. The developer does not sell or share extension data.

Redirect destinations

If you create a redirect rule, Chrome sends the redirected request to the destination you choose, subject to browser security rules. That destination may receive request information such as your IP address and applicable request headers or cookies. Its own privacy practices apply. Only configure destinations you trust.

Permissions

Access to HTTP/HTTPS websites and the webRequest permission allow the extension to observe request URLs. The declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess permission applies your block and redirect rules. The storage permission keeps your rules and settings locally and the recent request list in session storage.

Your controls and deletion

Remove individual rules in Existing rules or turn off the master switch to stop monitoring and interception. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data from the browser profile. Changing the active tab clears the recent request list. Keep sensitive URLs out of support messages.

Support and website

If you email the developer, the information you provide is used to respond to your request and is processed by the email provider. These static website pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages add no analytics or tracking cookies.
